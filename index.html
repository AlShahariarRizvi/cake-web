About Heavenly Bites

Heavenly Bites creates handcrafted cakes using premium ingredients, baked fresh in small batches and finished entirely by hand. From birthdays and weddings to anniversaries and quiet everyday celebrations, every cake we make is designed to deliver a little more happiness to your table.

  • Premium, locally-sourced ingredients
  • Custom flavours & designs for any occasion
  • Baked fresh to order, never frozen
